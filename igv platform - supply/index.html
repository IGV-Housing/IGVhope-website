What is IGVhope?
IGVhope is a homeownership program that converts finished strata units into a structured path to ownership for qualified residents — rather than sitting on the market as unsold inventory or being absorbed at a discount by institutional buyers.
Developers choose one of two paths: sell directly to IGVhope at a DSCR-underwritten price, or refinance up to 95% of value with provincial backing while retaining ownership. Either way, the units enter the Hope program and residents move in on a fixed price, structured toward ownership over a defined period.
The program is built on CMHC underwriting standards and current BC tax treatment, so every number is a real, actionable figure rather than a rough estimate.
