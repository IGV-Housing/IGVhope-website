Why housing support helps your workplace

Your people work here. Many can't afford to buy here.

Teachers, nurses, tradespeople, hospitality and retail teams: plenty of people with steady jobs and good habits are shut out by the down payment, not the monthly cost.

$790,800Typical (benchmark) price of a single-family home on Vancouver Island, June 2026.*
$158,000Cash needed for a 20% down payment on that home, on top of monthly costs.*
10+ yearsHow long saving a down payment can take for some households.**

What it looks like when people can't afford to stay

  • Hospitals struggle to hire and schools lose good teachers.
  • Businesses can't fill shifts.
  • Families spend more time driving and less time together.
  • People stop putting down roots, because they can't afford to.

Housing isn't just buildings. It is the people who make a town work every day.

Who IGVhope is for

  • People with a steady income who pay their bills.
  • People who can comfortably make regular monthly payments.
  • People who feel locked out of the traditional buying process.

It is not social housing or an investment product. It is for people who will live in the home.
