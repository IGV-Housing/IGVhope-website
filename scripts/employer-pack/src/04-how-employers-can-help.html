How employers can help

Six simple ways to back your team's journey to a home

Many people on your team have steady jobs and pay their bills, yet the down payment keeps homeownership out of reach. You can help them find out about IGVhope without spending a dollar. Pick whatever suits your workplace.

1

Share the word

Run the ready-made blurb in your staff newsletter or on your intranet, and add the banner to email signatures.

5 minutes
2

Put up the poster

Print the A4 noticeboard poster for the break room, the staff notice board or a shop window. The QR code links straight to IGVhope.

5 minutes
3

Make room for the check

The eligibility check takes about three minutes, and the free webinar lets staff pick a time that suits them. Let people know it's fine to take a few minutes for it.

Ongoing
4

Host a short chat

Invite staff to a lunch-and-learn. Email {{EMAIL}} to ask the IGVhope team about talking with your people.

About 1 hour
5

Name a contact

Pick someone in HR or management whom staff can talk to, and who can confirm employment when a team member asks as part of their application.

10 minutes
6

Tell other employers

Pass this pack to your chamber of commerce, business association or suppliers, so more teams in your community hear about it.

5 minutes

What this is not asking

  • No money from your business.
  • No guarantee, signature or endorsement from you. Staff decide for themselves.
  • No change to anyone's pay, benefits or contract.
  • No pressure on staff to take part. It simply makes sure they know it exists.
