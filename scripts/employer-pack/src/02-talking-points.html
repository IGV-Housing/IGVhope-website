For you, the participant

What IGVhope gives you

  • No down payment to move into a brand-new home.
  • Your price is set by independent valuation and held for the 10-year term.
  • A monthly occupancy contribution, with annual increases capped.
  • At Year 10, buy at the held price with your own mortgage. Approval isn't guaranteed, and the team helps you prepare.
  • A free 3-minute eligibility check, with no impact on your credit score.

For people who will live in the home. Not social housing or an investment.

For your employer

What's in it for your employer

  • Retention: people who can see a clear process to a home near work have a reason to stay.
  • Recruitment: something meaningful to point to when competing for good candidates.
  • A steadier team: staff who can plan for the long term.
  • Reputation: a visible, community-minded employer that backs its people.
  • A stronger community: a local workforce is also the local customer base and your neighbours.
