Employer FAQ

Questions employers ask

Straight answers before you share IGVhope with your team.

Does this cost our business anything?

No. Sharing information, putting up a poster or running a blurb is free. Registering, the eligibility check and the Homeownership Academy courses are free for your staff too.

What is IGVhope?

A new homeownership process on Vancouver Island. People move into a brand-new home with no down payment, the purchase price is set by independent valuation and held for ten years, and in Year 10 they buy the home with their own mortgage.

Are we endorsing it or responsible for it?

You are helping people find information. Each person decides for themselves, and should read the agreements with their own lawyer before signing anything.

Who qualifies?

People who can afford ongoing monthly housing costs but face barriers to a mortgage or down payment. It looks at stable income, ability to meet monthly occupancy contributions and a basic credit check similar to a rental application. Some communities may also have local employment or residency requirements.

Is it social housing or rent-to-own?

Neither. It is not social housing. And unlike many rent-to-own deals, there is no upfront payment and no extra amount set aside toward the price, so there is nothing extra at risk of being lost if life changes.

What happens at Year 10?

The goal is for the person to get their own mortgage and complete the purchase at the held price, and then ownership transfers to them. Approval is not guaranteed. If they are not mortgage-ready, the team reviews the options with them.

What if a team member's job changes?

They should contact the IGVhope team as soon as possible. Depending on the situation and the agreement, options may include short-term support, more time or a managed exit. The team works through it case by case.

Where are the homes?

Homes are planned and underway in Port Alberni, Campbell River and Ucluelet, with more communities being worked on across Vancouver Island, the BC Interior and beyond.
