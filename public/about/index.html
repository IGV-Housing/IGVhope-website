Why We Exist

Homeownership shouldn't move further away every year.

For generations, buying a home followed a simple formula.
  • Work hard.
  • Save.
  • Buy a home.
Today that process no longer works for millions of people.
  • House prices rise faster than incomes.
  • Down payments grow faster than savings.
  • The goal keeps moving.
IGVhope wasn't created to change the dream. We created it to change the process.
