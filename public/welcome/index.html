The program

How IGVhope works

Three simple steps. One clear process to ownership.

  1. 1

    Secure

    IGVhope is designed to hold your price steady, even if the market moves.

    See more

    Move into a brand-new home with no down payment required. When your home is ready to move in, an independent valuation holds the purchase price. The IGVhope program is built so that when your home becomes available to purchase, the price is held at that valuation, not the market price years later.

  2. 2

    Live

    Move in to your brand new home. Clear monthly payments and security.

    See more

    You pay a predictable monthly occupancy contribution, with annual increases capped, so you know what to expect. During the ten years, your home is held by a not-for-profit housing association. While you live there, we help you prepare for homeownership through annual check-ins, lending partner support, and the Homeownership Academy.

  3. 3

    Own

    Work toward your own mortgage, completing your purchase at year ten at your agreed price.

    See more

    The market may change, but your agreed purchase price is held for the ten-year term. Along the way, IGVhope helps you build a strong payment history and prepare for your transition to your mortgage. When your mortgage is in place at Year 10, title transfers to you, and you then own your home.

The communities

Port Alberni

Port Alberni is a vibrant and growing community where natural beauty and community spirit come together. Set between Mt. Arrowsmith and the picturesque Alberni Inlet, it offers breathtaking scenery and an abundance of outdoor adventures year-round. Beyond its stunning surroundings, Port Alberni is known for its friendly residents, strong community values, and growing opportunities for families and individuals to thrive. With excellent recreational facilities, local shops and restaurants, healthcare services, a hospital, schools, and cultural amenities, Port Alberni provides the perfect balance of connection, convenience, and lifestyle, making it an exceptional place to call home.

Ucluelet

Ucluelet, "Ukee", offers a unique balance of natural wonder, close community connection, and quality of life. Nestled along the stunning West Coast of Vancouver Island, this vibrant community is known for its dramatic ocean views, which include a brilliant "storm watching" season, lush rainforests, and endless opportunities to enjoy the outdoors. Residents are drawn not only to its spectacular surroundings but also to its welcoming atmosphere, strong community values, and slower pace of life. With access to local shops and services, schools, healthcare, recreation, and a thriving arts and tourism sector, Ucluelet provides the amenities needed for everyday living while maintaining its distinct coastal character. It is a place where individuals and families can establish roots, embrace a healthy lifestyle, and experience a true sense of home.

Your protections throughout the process

If life changes

IGVhope is designed for commitment. It is also designed for the reality that life does not always go to plan.

Support measures for income disruptionBuilt in from the start. If the unexpected happens we work with you on the next steps.
Your household changesTell us. Separations, new arrivals and people moving in or out all happen over ten years. Our team works through it with you one to one.
You need to leaveIf your circumstances change and you need to leave, our team works with you one to one on what that looks like for your situation.
You are not mortgage-readyIf you are not mortgage-ready as Year 10 approaches, our team works with you on your options, case by case.

Common questions

How is IGVhope different from rent-to-own?

Most rent-to-own models ask for an upfront payment, or bank part of each monthly payment as credit toward the purchase. If life changes, that money can be at risk. With IGVhope there is no down payment to start, and your monthly payments are not inflated to hold money toward the purchase price, so there is no extra payment at risk of being lost.

Who holds the home during the ten years?

A not-for-profit housing association, which sits separately from the company. It is there to protect your position rather than to profit from it. IGVhope is designed to help you be ready to complete your purchase at Year 10, when legal title transfers to you.

Can I buy an IGVhope home as an investment?

No. IGVhope is designed for people to own a home they will live in. We make no promise about what your home will be worth.

Can I choose which home I live in?

Our team will let you know options for you and timelines for construction.

Will there be renters in the building?

Alongside the ownership process, IGVhope aims to provide attainably priced rentals in the communities we build in, for renters and essential workers who need them. You get the same quality of home either way.

Can I have a pet?

Pets are part of a home, and your Occupancy Agreement sets out how they work. Tell us about your animals early and our team will tell you exactly where you stand.

What you agree to

Two agreements

Here is what each one does, in plain words.

Occupancy Agreement

Lets you live in the home. It works like a lease. It sets out your monthly occupancy contributions, the cap on annual increases for your home, which will not go above the BC maximum, and how things like pets work.

Ownership Agreement

Offered in Year 2, once your home has its own title. It sets out the agreed price and how you complete your purchase at Year 10.

Please read both with your own lawyer before you sign. Our team will go through them with you, so you know exactly where you stand before anything is signed.
