The IGVhope Process

Three simple steps. One clear process to ownership.

1
At move-in
Secure

IGVhope holds the purchase price even if the market changes over the 10-year term.

  • Move into a brand-new home with no down payment required.
  • When your home is ready to move into, an independent valuation establishes your agreed purchase price.
  • IGVhope is designed to protect that price for the ten-year term, giving you certainty even if the market moves.
Years 1 to 10
Live

Move into your brand-new home now while you prepare for ownership.

  • During the ten-year term, your home is held by a not-for-profit housing association.
  • Pay a monthly occupancy contribution, with annual increases capped under your agreement.
  • IGVhope supports your preparation for homeownership through annual check-ins, lending partner support and the Homeownership Academy.
Year 10
Own

Build toward your own mortgage and complete your purchase at the held price in year 10.

  • Your agreed purchase price is protected throughout the ten-year term, regardless of changes in market value.
  • IGVhope helps you build a strong payment history and prepare for your mortgage.
  • When your mortgage is in place at Year 10, title transfers to you and you own your home.

Common questions

How is IGVhope different from rent-to-own?

Most rent-to-own models ask for an upfront payment, or bank part of each monthly payment as credit toward the purchase. If life changes, that money can be at risk. With IGVhope there is no down payment to start, and your monthly payments are not inflated to hold money toward the purchase price, so there is no extra payment at risk of being lost.

Who holds the home during the ten years?

A not-for-profit housing association, which sits separately from the company. It is there to protect your position rather than to profit from it. IGVhope is designed to help you be ready to complete your purchase at Year 10, when legal title transfers to you.

Can I buy an IGVhope home as an investment?

No. IGVhope is designed for people to own a home they will live in. We make no promise about what your home will be worth.

Can I choose which home I live in?

Our team will let you know options for you and timelines for construction.

Will there be renters in the building?

Alongside the ownership process, IGVhope aims to provide attainably priced rentals in the communities we build in, for renters and essential workers who need them. You get the same quality of home either way.

Can I have a pet?

Pets are part of a home, and your Occupancy Agreement sets out how they work. Tell us about your animals early and our team will tell you exactly where you stand.

What you agree to

Two agreements

Here is what each one does, in plain words.

Agreement 1 · From move-in

Occupancy Agreement

Lets you live in the home. It works like a lease.

It sets out
  • Your monthly occupancy contributions
  • The cap on annual increases for your home, which will not go above the BC maximum
  • How things like pets work
Year 2
Agreement 2 · From Year 2

Ownership Agreement

Offered in Year 2, once your home has its own title.

It sets out
  • The agreed price for your home
  • How you complete your purchase at Year 10

We recommend reviewing both agreements with a lawyer, with our team on hand to help.
