Is IGVhope right for you?

IGVhope isn't for everyone, and that's by design. Take a look at both sides before you start the qualification process.

Who It's For

  • You have stable income, but the down payment keeps slipping out of reach.
  • You want to move into your home now, not spend years saving before you can.
  • You're ready to commit to a clear ten-year path toward full ownership.
  • You value predictability: a locked purchase price and terms that don't move on you.
  • You're looking for a primary residence to live in.

Who It's Not For

  • You're looking for a short-term rental with no ownership goal.
  • You want to renovate a fixer-upper, not move into a brand-new build.
  • You're purchasing as an investment, not a place to actually live.
  • You aren't ready to make a long-term commitment.
  • You expect zero involvement in the process. This is a partnership, not a hand-out.
