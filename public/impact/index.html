Economic Impact

Roughly $561 million in direct construction investment, every year.

Every IGVhope home is a home under construction somewhere on Vancouver Island: materials bought, trades hired, local suppliers paid. At our target of 2,000 homes a year, that's the scale of direct construction investment we're building toward.

How we calculated this

Based on Statistics Canada's 2025 average construction value per residential dwelling unit (≈$280,700, national Building Permits data), applied to our target annual volume. This is direct construction value only. It excludes the indirect and induced spending that typically follows a housing project through a local economy, so if anything it understates the eventual figure.

Employment Impact

Approximately 4,000 jobs supported a year.

Framers, electricians, plumbers, project managers, and the local trades and suppliers around them, depend on a steady pipeline of work. A ten-year program at scale is designed to give them exactly that, rather than the boom-bust cycle of a single project.

How we calculated this

National data from the Canadian Home Builders' Association shows new-home construction supported roughly 515,000 jobs against 259,000 housing starts in 2025, about two jobs per home, direct and closely related indirect. Applied to our target of 2,000 homes a year, that's an approximate 4,000 jobs a year at full scale.

Family Stability & Community Focus

Around 2,000 families a year, putting down roots instead of chasing rent.

The real impact isn't a dollar figure: it's what a locked-in ten-year path to ownership does for a household that no longer moves at a landlord's notice. Same school, same friends, same street, and the kind of long-term local ties a transient rental market makes hard to build.

How we calculated this

Modelled directly against our target of 2,000 keys delivered a year, one household stabilized per home delivered at full scale.

Affordable Rentals

One affordable rental in every Osprey we build.

The Osprey comes in configurations of 4 to 6 homes, depending on local planning rules. One home in every Osprey IGVhope aims to set aside as an affordable rental to meet community needs.

How we calculated this

Across a target delivery of 2,000 homes a year, one-in-every-Osprey works out to approximately 400 new affordable rental homes entering the market annually, for people who need a rental now rather than a ten-year ownership path.
