Your ownership pathway
Now - Year 1
Move in
You pay capped monthly occupancy rates. A portion goes toward building your future equity stake. No mortgage qualification required at entry.
Year 1 - 5
Credit building & pre-approval
Work with our lending partners to strengthen your credit profile and build savings. IGVhope navigators check in quarterly.
Year 5 - 10
Transition window opens
You become eligible to purchase your home at the originally agreed price - protected from market appreciation during your tenancy.
Year 10
Ownership transfer
Transition to your mortgage. The equity from the market change during the 10 years becomes yours. You are a homeowner.
