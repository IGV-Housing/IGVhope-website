Housing Context
Rental prices are high because housing stock is short. When supply is constrained, everyone loses: renters pay more, working households can't get ahead, and communities stagnate. Meeting this challenge doesn't require government handouts or utopian thinking. It requires practical models that work commercially and deliver homes faster. The HOPE program is built to do exactly that.
